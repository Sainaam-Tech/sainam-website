### Step 1 — Download & Install

1. Tap the **Download APK** button above on your Android phone
2. Once downloaded, open the file from your notifications or Downloads folder
3. Android will show a warning — tap **Settings** and enable **Install from unknown sources** for your browser or file manager
4. Tap **Install** and wait a few seconds
5. Open **LR Mitra** from your home screen

> **Note:** This security step is needed because the app is distributed outside the Play Store. It is safe — built and maintained by Sainam Technology.

---

### Step 2 — Register Your Account

1. Open the app and enter your **10-digit mobile number**
2. Tap **Send OTP** — you will receive an SMS or WhatsApp message
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP and tap **Verify**
4. Your account is created. No password needed.

---

### Step 3 — Set Up Your Organisation

1. After login, tap **Create Organisation**
2. Enter your **transport company name** (e.g. "Raut Transport")
3. Select your **vehicle number** (optional but recommended)
4. Tap **Save** — your organisation is ready

---

### Step 4 — Create Your First Lorry Receipt

1. From the home screen tap **+ New LR**
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Consignor** — who is sending the goods
   - **Consignee** — who is receiving the goods
   - **From / To** — origin and destination city
   - **Material** — what is being transported
   - **Weight & Freight** — quantity and charges
3. Tap **Save LR** — your LR is created with an auto-generated LR number
4. Tap **Share** to send the LR as PDF via WhatsApp or any app

---

### Step 5 — Manage Your LRs

- **History** — view all past LRs with search and filter
- **Parties** — your saved consignors and consignees auto-fill next time
- **Team** — add staff members on the Business plan (up to 10 users)
- **Subscription** — upgrade or manage your plan from the Profile section

Pump Mitra helps petrol pump owners run a secure, offer-based fuel transaction system. The pump operator creates offers for specific vehicles. When a vehicle fuels up under an active offer, the operator generates a one-time QR code on their phone — valid for a very short window and single-use only. The vehicle user scans it to complete the transaction. No static QR codes stuck to walls, no room for fraud.

---

### Step 1 — Register Your Pump

1. Go to [pump-mitra.sainam.tech](https://pump-mitra.sainam.tech) on your phone or computer
2. Click **Register** and enter your mobile number
3. Verify with OTP
4. Enter your **pump name**, **location**, and **owner details**
5. Your pump account is created

---

### Step 2 — Create an Offer for a Vehicle

1. From your dashboard, go to **Offers → New Offer**
2. Select or add the **vehicle number** the offer applies to
3. Set the offer details — fuel amount, discount, or any condition
4. Save the offer — it is now active for that vehicle

---

### Step 3 — Generate a One-Time QR When Vehicle Arrives

1. When the vehicle arrives and fuels under the active offer, open the offer on your phone
2. Tap **Generate QR** — a unique QR code is created on the operator's screen
3. The QR is **valid for a very short time** and can be **scanned only once**
4. Show or present the QR to the vehicle user to scan with their phone
5. Once scanned, the QR expires immediately — cannot be reused or shared

---

### Step 4 — Vehicle User Scans the QR

1. Vehicle user opens their phone camera and scans the QR shown by the operator
2. The transaction is recorded instantly — no app download needed for the user
3. Confirmation appears on both sides

---

### Step 5 — Reports & History

- **Per-vehicle reports** — full transaction history for any specific vehicle across any date range
- **All-vehicle reports** — overview of all transactions across your pump for any period
- **Period filters** — daily, weekly, monthly, or custom date range
- **Offer performance** — see how many times each offer was used and by which vehicles
